Coach TCF Canada · Tâche 3

Write your answer, then fix your own mistakes.

How it works

  1. Write the topic, then your full answer in French: at least 300 words (about 3 minutes spoken) and at least 5 connectors. Send them together in one message.
  2. Follow this plan: introduction (the two opinions) → my opinion → argument 1 → argument 2 → problem → solution → conclusion.
  3. The coach first checks your length, connectors and plan. Then it marks your mistakes. You correct them yourself, and it won't give you the answer.
  4. Sometimes it asks you to explain an idea in English.
  5. At the end, you get your 3 most frequent errors and your corrected version. Learn it by heart and say it aloud without reading.
